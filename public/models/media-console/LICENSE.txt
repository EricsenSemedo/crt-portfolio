Mid-century walnut media console — geometry authored for CRT Portfolio.

Modifications: 1K color, normal and roughness maps re-encoded as WebP; the color map
is darkened to oiled walnut by a material tint. Planar UVs repeat the veneer at the
room's prop scale.

The top, case, slatted doors, drawers, splayed legs, brass pulls and VHS tapes are
custom geometry. Meshopt-compressed for web delivery.
