Modern residential garage — geometry authored for CRT Portfolio.
Rebuild source: scripts/build-modern-garage.py

Surface asset: Painted Plaster Wall — Amal Kumar / Poly Haven
https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_plaster_wall
Downloaded 2026-09-08. CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Modifications: 1K JPEG color/roughness maps, 512px JPEG normal map;
repeating UVs at the source two-meter scale, reduced normal strength.

The garage shell, sectional door, curved tracks, hinges, opener, ceiling,
cabinets, lights and floor are custom geometry. No third-party garage model
or BlenderKit royalty-free geometry is included in this asset.
Static geometry merged by material and Meshopt-compressed for web delivery.
